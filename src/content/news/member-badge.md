Members with a current, paid-up HAZ membership can now add the HAZ member badge to their website, email signature and proposals.

The badge links to your hotel's profile in the HAZ member directory, and it expires with the membership year.

Use the dark badge on light or white backgrounds, and the light badge on dark backgrounds. Show it 160 px wide on screen, never smaller than 140 px.
